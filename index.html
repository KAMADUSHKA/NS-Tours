        About NS Travel And Tours

        Explore Sri Lanka with a Trusted Local Team from Hikkaduwa

        NS Travel And Tours is a local tour agency on Galle Road in Narigama, Hikkaduwa, right on Sri Lanka’s south coast. We plan and run day tours, wildlife safaris and water adventures for visitors from around the world, and our guests rate us 4.6 ★ from 78 reviews on Google.

        Because we’re based on the coast, the best of the south is close by, and the island’s most famous sights are just a day trip away.

        • Half-day trips near Hikkaduwa: Madu River safari, Galle Fort, Nilwala River crocodiles and an elephant experience.
        • Full one-day tours: Ella, Yala, Kandy, Ambuluwawa, Sigiriya, Sinharaja Rainforest and Colombo.
        • Water sports & ocean adventures: surfing lessons, snorkelling, scuba and wreck diving, deep-sea fishing and more.
        • Easy booking: message us directly on WhatsApp at +94 70 305 0177 to plan your day.
